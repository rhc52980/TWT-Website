Public Health Alert

Legionella Risk & Regulatory Compliance

Cooling towers and evaporative condensers generate water aerosols that, if contaminated with Legionella pneumophila, can transmit Legionnaires' disease over several miles.

State health departments and safety regulatory agencies enforce strict water safety standards. Under ASHRAE Standard 188, commercial and industrial building owners are responsible for maintaining a documented Water Safety Plan and proving disinfection efficacy.

Biocides are Not Enough Regular chemical dosing alone cannot pierce protective bacterial biofilms. Physical sanitization and chemistry management are required.
